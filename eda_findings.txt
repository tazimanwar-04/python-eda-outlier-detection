EDA FINDINGS – HOUSE PRICE DATASET (King County)

1. Dataset Overview:
- The dataset contains 21,613 rows and 21 columns.
- No missing values were found in the dataset.
- The 'date' column was converted to proper datetime format.

2. Price Distribution:
- The price distribution is right-skewed.
- Most houses are priced between approximately 300,000 and 650,000.
- The maximum price before treatment was 7,700,000.

3. Outlier Detection:
- Outliers were detected using the IQR (Interquartile Range) method.
- Q1 (25th percentile) = 321,950
- Q3 (75th percentile) = 645,000
- IQR = 323,050
- Upper bound = 1,129,575
- Total outliers detected = 1,159

4. Outlier Handling:
- Instead of removing outliers, price values above the upper bound were capped at 1,129,575.
- This method preserved data while controlling extreme influence.
- An additional column 'price_outlier_flag' was created to mark original outliers.

5. Correlation Analysis:
- Strongest positive correlations with price:
    - Grade (0.706)
    - Sqft_living (0.695)
    - Sqft_living15 (0.629)
    - Sqft_above (0.606)
    - Bathrooms (0.535)

- This indicates that house quality and living area size are the most influential factors in determining price.

6. Key Insights:
- Larger and higher-quality houses are significantly more expensive.
- Bedrooms have weaker correlation compared to total living area.
- Luxury houses exist in the dataset but were statistically treated using IQR capping.

Conclusion:
The dataset was successfully analyzed using EDA techniques including distribution analysis, IQR-based outlier detection, capping, and correlation analysis. The cleaned dataset is ready for further modeling or machine learning tasks.
